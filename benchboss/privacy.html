BenchBoss Hockey Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Developer: Whistle, Raven & Footnote LLC (“we,” “us”)
App: BenchBoss Hockey (com.whistleravenfootnote.benchboss.hockey)
Contact: WhistleRavenAndFootnote@gmail.com · whistleravenandfootnote.com

BenchBoss Hockey is a hockey drill drawing and practice planning app for coaches. It is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children. This policy covers BenchBoss Hockey only; our other apps are covered by our main Privacy Policy.

The short version

  • The app works offline. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We run no servers for this app. Your drills, practices, videos, and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Dictation uses your device's on-device speech recognition. No audio leaves your device.
  • The app shows small banner ads served by Google AdMob, in both the free and the Pro version. The free version also shows an occasional full-screen ad between sections. Google collects some data to do that (described below).
  • Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

1. What stays on your device

The app turns off Android's Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer for itself, so your drills, practices, and settings are not backed up and are not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone by Android's backup or phone-to-phone transfer. They exist only on this device. To move to a new phone, export your drills and practices with Share first.

Your drawings (.drill files), practices (.practice files), video reviews, your settings, a center-ice logo image you choose, and a cached record that you own Pro are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive, copy, or back up any of this. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from your device. Use Share to keep a copy yourself.

2. Sharing and importing

The app only shares or opens files when you ask it to. Exporting a drawing or practice, sending a practice sheet, or sending a video review uses Android's share sheet: you choose where the file goes (for example, email or a messaging app), and that destination's own privacy policy applies.

Importing uses Android's file picker or “Open with”: the app reads only the file you choose. The center-ice logo is chosen with Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the image you pick. The app never uploads files by itself.

3. Video review

Video review lets you draw on game video. You choose the video yourself with Android's picker. Depending on how you add it, the app either links to the video where it already is on your device (your copy in the gallery is not changed) or saves its own copy in the app's private storage. Your drawings, moments, and notes on the video are stored on your device.

When you send a video review, the app packages the review and its video into a single file and hands it to Android's share sheet; you choose where it goes. When someone sends you a review, the app unpacks it into its private storage on your device. Deleting a review deletes the app's own copy of the video; a video linked from your gallery is not touched. Videos are never uploaded to us, and no ads are shown on the video review screen.

4. Dictation (microphone)

If you tap the microphone button, the app asks for the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) permission and uses Android's on-device speech recognition to turn what you say into text in the drill description. The app requires on-device recognition: if your device cannot recognize speech on-device, the app says so and does nothing else. It does not fall back to cloud recognition.

We do not record, store, or transmit audio. The resulting text is saved in your drill on your device, like any other text you type.

5. Ads

The free and the Pro version show small banner ads on the list screens (the home screen, Plays, Drills, Practices, and the Video review list), served by Google AdMob. There are no ads on the drawing canvas, the practice page, while a practice is running, on the video review screen, or in Settings.

Free users may also see an occasional full-screen ad between sections (at most one every few minutes, never in the first minute). Pro users, and free users while drawing, running a practice, or reviewing video, never see one.

Before requesting ads, the app shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) where required, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and some US states. You can change your choice later in the app where the consent form is offered, or in your Android settings. If you decline, ads may be non-personalized or not shown.

When ads are requested, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use, according to Google's own policies:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes, performance).

We do not receive this data as a personal record of you; we may see aggregated ad performance in our AdMob account. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the AdMob data disclosure.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether your Google account owns Pro. We do not see your name, card, or other payment details. The app stores a cached “Pro” flag on your device so Pro keeps working offline, and re-checks with Google Play when it is online. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, Pro turns off; your drills are never deleted. Google's terms and privacy policy govern the purchase.

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: used by Google Play Billing and by AdMob. The app's drawing, practice, video, and library features need no internet.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob for ads.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): only for on-device dictation, only when you tap the microphone button.
  • Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): keeps the screen on while you run a practice.
  • Google Play Billing: to buy and restore Pro.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, phone, SMS, or device-wide storage permissions.

8. What we do not do

We do not have accounts, collect your name or email, track your location, sell your data, use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own, or use your data for anything other than what is described here.

9. Children

BenchBoss Hockey is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age required in your country).

10. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete: delete files inside the app or uninstall it. For ad data held by Google, use Android's ad settings, the in-app consent form, or Google Ads Settings. If you are in the EEA, the UK, California, or elsewhere with privacy rights, you can contact us at WhistleRavenAndFootnote@gmail.com and we will help as far as the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the dates above and publish the new version at this URL. If a change affects what the app collects, the app update notes will say so.

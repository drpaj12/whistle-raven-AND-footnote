About the app

Flip the roles

Role reversal is a practice technique in which partners swap places, so the person who usually listens also gets to experience the other side of a conversation. FlipTheMic makes that exercise simple to set up and run, whether in a classroom, a clinic, or at home.

It was built for people who stutter and the people around them: speech-language pathology students learning about stuttering, clinicians, family members, and friends who want to understand it better.

Privacy

Nothing is recorded. Ever.

FlipTheMic does not record, save, upload, or share any audio, voice, or speech. Any sound the app handles is processed in real time on your device and discarded immediately. Nobody, including us, can listen to or retrieve what is said.

  • No account, no sign-in, and no personal information collected by us
  • Free, supported by a small banner ad with no pop-ups or video
  • Ads never have access to the microphone

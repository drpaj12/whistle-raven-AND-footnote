FlipTheMic

About the app

Flip the roles

Most people only ever hear stuttering from the listener's side. FlipTheMic flips that around. It puts you behind the mic, so you can experience for yourself what it feels like when speech doesn't come out the way you planned.

It is built for anyone who wants to understand stuttering better: speech-language pathology students and instructors, clinicians, teachers, and the family members, friends, and colleagues of people who stutter. It is easy to use in a classroom, a clinic, or at home.

Privacy

Nothing is recorded. Ever.

FlipTheMic does not record, save, upload, or share any audio, voice, or speech. Any sound the app handles is processed in real time on your device and discarded immediately. Nobody, including us, can listen to or retrieve what is said.

  • No account, no sign-in, and no personal information collected by us
  • Free, supported by a small banner ad with no pop-ups or video
  • Ads never have access to the microphone

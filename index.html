Services

What we do

Software Development

Custom tools, applications and prototypes, from first sketch to working software. We build small, sturdy systems that do what they need to do.

Technical Consulting

An outside expert view on architecture, technology choices, feasibility and technical risk, so you can decide with confidence.

Research & Prototyping

Turning an idea or a paper into something you can run. We do quick, honest experiments that show whether an approach is worth pursuing.

Documentation & Review

Code reviews, technical writing and audits. We leave a clear record of how things work and why they were built that way.

Approach

How we work

  1. Listen

    We start with your problem and your constraints, not with a technology.

  2. Scope

    We agree on a plan in writing, with clear deliverables, milestones and a price.

  3. Build

    We work in small steps you can check along the way. No surprises at the end.

  4. Hand off

    You get working results, the source, and the documentation needed to carry on without us.

About

A small studio in Oxford, Ohio

Whistle, Raven & Footnote was founded in 2026 by Peter Jamieson, a software creator, game maker, and hockey referee and coach. The whistle in the name is the real thing.

The studio makes what it knows: tools for the bench, built by someone who stands behind it; games that are fun to play; and software made with experts in their field. Every app is made carefully, down to the footnotes.

1. Who we are

Whistle, Raven & Footnote LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is an Ohio limited liability company based in Oxford, Ohio, USA. We make the apps listed below and operate this website, whistleravenandfootnote.com.

2. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • BenchBoss, a hockey app for drawing plays and planning practices. It is free with ads, and an optional BenchBoss Pro upgrade removes its full-screen ads. BenchBoss is covered by its own BenchBoss Hockey Privacy Policy, which takes priority over this policy for that app;
  • FlipTheMic, a free, ad-supported role-reversal app that lets people experience what stuttering feels like;
  • Two-Stick SportsBall, a single-player sports game made for all ages, including children. The free version includes a half-season of play with child-directed ads, and an optional one-time Full Game Unlock removes the ads and unlocks full seasons; and
  • this website.

Together these are our “Services.”

3. Information we collect

We, Whistle, Raven & Footnote LLC, do not collect personal information through our apps. None of our apps:

  • requires or offers an account, sign-in, or registration;
  • asks for your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details;
  • accesses your contacts, photos, files, or precise (GPS) location; or
  • sends your content, or information about what you do in the app, to us.

Our apps show ads provided by Google AdMob. Google, not us, collects certain device and usage data to deliver those ads, as described in Advertising. In our children's game this collection is restricted, as described in Children's privacy.

The only personal information we receive directly is what you choose to send us, for example when you email us for support (see Contact us), and the limited purchase records that Google Play provides (see Purchases).

4. App-by-app details

BenchBoss

BenchBoss lets you draw hockey plays and build practice plans. The plays, drills, practice plans, rosters, notes, and settings you create are saved only on your device. We cannot see them, they are not uploaded to us, and they are never shared with advertisers. If you choose to share or export something (for example, sending a practice plan to another coach), it goes through the sharing method you pick, such as your email or messaging app, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

BenchBoss shows small banner ads on its list screens in both the free and Pro versions, and the free version also shows an occasional full-screen ad between sections (see Advertising). BenchBoss Pro removes the full-screen ads.

BenchBoss Hockey has its own detailed privacy policy, covering video review, on-device dictation, and Google's consent form: the BenchBoss Hockey Privacy Policy.

FlipTheMic

FlipTheMic is free to download and is supported by ads (see Advertising).

Nothing is recorded. FlipTheMic does not record, store, save, upload, transmit, or share any audio, voice, speech, video, or transcript. If the app uses your device's microphone for a feature, any sound is handled only in real time on your device and is discarded straight away. It is never written to storage and never leaves your device. Nobody, including us and our advertising partner, can listen to or retrieve anything said while using FlipTheMic. Ads have no access to the microphone or to anything you say.

FlipTheMic does not collect health information or information about your speech, and nothing about how you use its practice features is shared with advertisers. Ads in FlipTheMic are limited to small banners so they do not interrupt practice. Any settings you change stay on your device.

Two-Stick SportsBall

Two-Stick SportsBall is a single-player game made for all ages, including children. It has no online multiplayer, chat, user-generated content sharing, or social features, and no analytics or tracking. Game progress, scores, and settings are saved only on your device.

The free version includes a half-season of play and shows child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated ads only. The game does not collect or transmit the device's advertising ID. The one-time Full Game Unlock removes the ads and unlocks full seasons. See Children's privacy for details.

5. Advertising

The free versions of our apps show ads using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. We do not receive any information that identifies you. To deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into our apps sends certain information from your device to Google, such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Not the advertising ID in our children's game;
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address (not GPS);
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • Diagnostic and device information, such as device model, operating system, app version, and ad performance and crash data.

Google uses this information as described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices about ads:

  • Remove ads: buy the Full Game Unlock in Two-Stick SportsBall, and the game no longer requests or shows ads. BenchBoss Pro removes BenchBoss's full-screen ads; small banners remain on its list screens.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland): in BenchBoss and FlipTheMic, you will be asked the first time you open the app whether you consent to personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the app's privacy settings.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, under Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording may vary by device). You can also manage Google ad settings at My Ad Center. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Ads are provided by third parties. We use Google's controls to block inappropriate categories, but we do not choose individual ads. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, especially in our children's game, please tell us.

6. Data stored on your device

Our apps save your content and settings locally on your device so they are there next time you open the app. This data is under your control. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include app data in a backup to your Google account. Google manages that backup under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. We do not have access to it.

7. Purchases

BenchBoss offers an optional one-time “BenchBoss Pro” upgrade that unlocks extra features and removes full-screen ads (small banner ads remain on list screens). Two-Stick SportsBall offers an optional one-time “Full Game Unlock” that removes ads and unlocks full seasons beyond the free half-season. Both are in-app purchases, and these are the only purchases in our apps. All payments are processed by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and Google Privacy Policy.

We never receive your payment card or bank details. Google provides us with limited transaction records, such as an order number, the product purchased, the date, the price, and general location information needed for tax purposes. We use these records only for accounting, tax, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and handling refund or support requests. The app checks with Google Play on your device to confirm whether you have made the purchase.

8. Children's privacy

Two-Stick SportsBall is made for all ages, including children, and follows the Google Play Families Policy. BenchBoss and FlipTheMic are intended for teens and adults and are not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. In Two-Stick SportsBall:

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children, which tells Google to show only non-personalized ads that comply with COPPA and Google's Families ad policies. There is no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences, using Google's maximum ad content rating of “G”.
  • The game does not collect or transmit the Android advertising ID or other persistent identifiers for advertising. Google receives only the limited technical information needed to deliver the ad, measure it, and prevent fraud.
  • There is no chat, no sharing, and no way for a child to give personal information to us or to other players.
  • The one-time Full Game Unlock removes ads entirely and unlocks full seasons. It is purchased through Google Play, and parents can require approval for purchases, or block them, using Google Play parental controls and Google Family Link.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. In BenchBoss and FlipTheMic, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends device data, such as your advertising ID, to Google for advertising as described in Advertising. Under some U.S. state laws, this may count as “sharing” for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. You can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ads personalization in your device settings, or by emailing us. The ads on this website may also count as “sharing” under those laws; you can opt out as described in Ads on this website. Our children's game does not share data for targeted advertising. Apart from that, we disclose information only if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply to any information transferred.

This includes residents of California and other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and elsewhere. We usually hold no personal information about you, because our apps do not send any to us. For data that Google collects through ads, you can use the controls in Advertising and your Google Account. If you have emailed us or made a purchase and want to make a request, contact us and we will respond within the time required by law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Security

Our apps keep your content on your device and do not transmit it to us, which minimizes risk. We protect any emails or purchase records we hold with reasonable safeguards and keep them only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

1. Who we are

Whistle, Raven & Footnote LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is an Ohio limited liability company based in Oxford, Ohio, USA. We make the apps listed below and operate this website, whistleravenandfootnote.com.

2. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • BenchBoss, a hockey app for drawing plays and planning practices, including BenchBoss Pro;
  • FlipTheMic, a free role-reversal practice app for people who stutter and the people who work with them;
  • Cuber's Universe – 2 Stick Sports Ball, a single-player sports video game, including its Pro upgrade; and
  • this website.

Together these are our “Services.” If an app has its own privacy notice, that notice adds to this policy.

3. Information we collect

Through our apps, we do not collect any personal information. Specifically, our apps do not:

  • require or offer an account, sign-in, or registration;
  • ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details;
  • collect your location, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers;
  • use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services that collect data about you;
  • show advertisements or use advertising identifiers; or
  • send information about you or your use of the app to us or to anyone else.

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us, for example when you email us for support (see Contact us), and the limited purchase records that Google Play provides (see Purchases).

4. App-by-app details

BenchBoss

BenchBoss lets you draw hockey plays and build practice plans. The plays, drills, practice plans, rosters, notes, and settings you create are saved only on your device. We cannot see them and they are not uploaded to us. If you choose to share or export something (for example, sending a practice plan to another coach), it goes through the sharing method you pick, such as your email or messaging app, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

FlipTheMic

FlipTheMic is free and does not collect any information.

Nothing is recorded. FlipTheMic does not record, store, save, upload, transmit, or share any audio, voice, speech, video, or transcript. If the app uses your device's microphone for a feature, any sound is handled only in real time on your device and is discarded straight away. It is never written to storage and never leaves your device. Nobody, including us, can listen to or retrieve anything said while using FlipTheMic.

FlipTheMic does not collect health information or information about your speech. Any settings you change stay on your device.

Cuber's Universe – 2 Stick Sports Ball

Cuber's Universe – 2 Stick Sports Ball is a single-player game designed to be suitable for children. It has no online multiplayer, chat, user-generated content sharing, social features, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Game progress, scores, and settings are saved only on your device. See also Children's privacy.

5. Data stored on your device

Our apps save your content and settings locally on your device so they are there next time you open the app. This data is under your control. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include app data in a backup to your Google account. Google manages that backup under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. We do not have access to it.

6. Purchases

BenchBoss and Cuber's Universe – 2 Stick Sports Ball are paid apps and offer an optional “Pro” upgrade as an in-app purchase. All payments are processed by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and Google Privacy Policy.

We never receive your payment card or bank details. Google provides us with limited transaction records, such as an order number, the product purchased, the date, the price, and general location information needed for tax purposes. We use these records only for accounting, tax, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and handling refund or support requests. The app checks with Google Play on your device to confirm whether you have unlocked Pro.

7. Children's privacy

Cuber's Universe – 2 Stick Sports Ball is intended for a general audience that includes children under 13. Our other apps are not directed at children but may be used by them.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Our apps do not let children share personal information publicly or with other users, contain no advertising, and do not use third-party analytics or tracking.

In-app purchases require a Google Play purchase confirmation. Parents and guardians can require approval for purchases, or block them, using Google Play parental controls and Google Family Link.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. This website

This website has no sign-in, no cookies of our own, no analytics, and no advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may automatically log technical information such as your IP address for security and operational purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address so it can deliver them (see the Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive or use this information to identify you.

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We disclose information only if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply to any information transferred.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. This includes residents of California and other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and elsewhere. Because our apps do not collect personal information, we usually hold none about you. If you have emailed us or made a purchase and want to make a request, contact us and we will respond within the time required by law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Our apps keep your content on your device and do not transmit it, which minimizes risk. We protect any emails or purchase records we hold with reasonable safeguards and keep them only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new apps or features. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above. If an app ever begins collecting personal information, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety information before that change takes effect and, where required, ask for consent, including verifiable parental consent for children.

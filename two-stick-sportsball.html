The story

Welcome aboard the Ship

You leave Earth and head out toward the edge of the solar system, and there you learn the truth. Earth, the Sun, and every planet are one small part of Cuber's Universe, a ship so vast that whole solar systems are bolted to its hull.

The Ship runs on the energy of their suns. Each system is a breeding ground where Algorithmic and Alien Intelligences evolve, and the Ship harvests the ideas they produce. Useful intelligences are kept. The rest are recycled.

On board, the only way to prove you are worth keeping is to be good at the Ship's favorite game. Luckily, you didn't come alone. GnuBNNMS, the companion who traveled with you from Earth, is your teammate. He is mostly helpful, and he has opinions about everything.

“Relegation is a performance review with a furnace.” — GnuBNNMS

How it plays

Two sticks. One arena.

Two thumb sticks drive everything: the left stick flies your ship and the right stick aims and shoots. Your ship drifts with real momentum, so every turn and every stop is part of the skill.

  • Fly 2-on-2 with GnuBNNMS against rival clubs: pass, dodge, shoot, and score in the nets.
  • Every opponent is an AI from its own solar system, and every home arena plays differently: corner nets, pulsing no-shoot zones, near-frictionless floors, and arenas so dark you can barely see the goal.
  • Robots get in everyone's way. Blast them, dodge them, or let them wreck the other team's plans.
  • A real league table with promotion and relegation across three divisions. Climb the table, survive the playoffs, move up, and win it all.
  • Spend your winnings on upgrades and power-ups. Prize money is earned by playing and can never be bought.

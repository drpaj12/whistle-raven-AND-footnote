About the game

Climb the league

In Two-Stick SportsBall you fly a ship alongside your AI teammate, chasing the ball and trying to score in the nets. Every opponent is an AI team with its own home arena, so each away game plays out on new ground.

The league works the way real football leagues do, with promotion and relegation. Finish near the top to move up a division, slip to the bottom and you drop down. Work your way up through the divisions and win it all.

It is made by a referee and coach, so it is built around good sport: no violence, no trash talk, and no online strangers. It works just as well for a kid on a car ride as for a grown-up on a lunch break.
